## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-SEC08 | Denial of Service / Elevation of Privilege | `POST /auth/login` | mitigate | Per-route `config.rateLimit { max: 5, timeWindow: '15 minutes' }` keyed by IP (D-20/D-21); asserted by `tests/auth/rate-limit.test.ts` against the 6th attempt |
